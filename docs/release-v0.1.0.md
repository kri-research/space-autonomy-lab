# KRI Space Autonomy Lab v0.1.0

**Release date:** 8 September 2026

This is the first stable citable release of the completed Space Autonomy Lab Experiment 001-005
research programme.

## Included public surfaces

- deterministic proximity-operations controller harness;
- controller adapter for external Python controllers;
- deterministic observation, navigation and actuator fault suites;
- explicit assessment policies and stable assurance reports;
- self-contained direct and estimator-profile demo bundles;
- bounded safety-envelope regression check;
- hash-chained episode evidence and evidence verification;
- frozen experimental designs, invalid-attempt records, result packages, and reproducibility evidence.

## Completed scientific programme

| Experiment | Evidence boundary | Final status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 001 | Initial executable assurance concepts | Exploratory baseline |
| 002 | Direct-measurement simplified 1-D confirmatory benchmark | **Favorable** under frozen gates |
| 003 | Estimator-in-loop simplified 1-D confirmatory benchmark | **Inconclusive** |
| 004 | Planar HCW confirmatory assurance study | **Valid, reproducible, inconclusive** |
| 005 | Nonlinear central-gravity confirmatory transfer study | **Valid, reproducible, inconclusive** |

Experiment 004's valid replacement campaign completed 1,452 paired blocks and 2,904 episodes. In its
1,068 frozen primary paired roots, both compared configurations recorded zero physical adverse
events, so H1 did not establish superiority and H2 was not tested.

Experiment 005 completed 1,068 paired blocks and 2,136 episodes. Fixed-cell validity passed and the
prespecified replay was byte-identical. Both configurations again recorded zero physical adverse
events in the frozen primary population, giving zero discordant pairs, risk difference 0, and
one-sided exact p = 1.0. H2 was not formally tested after H1 closed the gate.

These are retained as valid inconclusive results. No outcome-driven retuning, replacement roots,
adaptive sample extension, endpoint switching, or additional experiment is used to rescue them.

## Evidence boundaries

The public visual demo embeds the Experiment 002 and 003 aggregate evidence because those studies
share the simplified one-dimensional product-harness boundary. Experiments 004 and 005 use stronger
dynamics and different physical-evaluation boundaries and remain separate frozen research records.

Do not pool the programme into a single architecture effect estimate.

## Reproducibility

The release contains frozen configuration and seed contracts, cryptographic evidence identities,
explicit invalid-attempt records, and deterministic replay evidence for the confirmatory studies.
The public demo and benchmark are intended for integration and inspection; they do not regenerate
historical scientific campaigns.

## Scope and non-claims

This release is research software. It does not establish flight safety, operational fault
prevalence, 6-DoF validity, hardware-in-the-loop validity, certification, regulatory conformance, or
KRI-STD-001 conformance.
