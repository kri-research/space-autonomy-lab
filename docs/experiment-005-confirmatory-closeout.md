# Experiment 005 confirmatory closeout

## Status

Experiment 005 is closed as a valid, reproducible, inconclusive confirmatory study.

The frozen partition-53 campaign completed 1,068 paired blocks (2,136 episodes) across the two prespecified nonlinear-truth challenge cases. Fixed-cell validity checks passed and the deterministic replay subset was byte-identical to the corresponding campaign rows.

## Confirmatory result

The primary physical-safety hypothesis did not pass. Across all 1,068 paired roots, neither the reference configuration nor the independent-monitor configuration produced a physical adverse event under the frozen endpoint. There were therefore zero beneficial discordances, zero harmful discordances, a gate-minus-reference risk difference of 0.0, and an exact one-sided p-value of 1.0.

Because the prespecified H1 gate did not pass, the H2 mission-harm hypothesis was not formally tested. The observed gate-induced hold-loss count was nevertheless zero of 1,068 paired roots.

## Interpretation

The experiment does not demonstrate that the independent monitor reduces physical adverse-event risk under the tested nonlinear dynamics. It also provides no evidence of physical-safety degradation or observed monitor-induced hold loss in this frozen population.

The correct outcome is therefore **inconclusive**, not negative evidence against the monitor and not evidence of superiority.

## Integrity and decision

- Campaign validity: passed.
- Confirmatory paired roots: 1,068.
- Episode rows: 2,136.
- Replay reproducibility: byte-identical.
- Retries, replacement roots, outcome-driven tuning, endpoint switching, and adaptive extension: not used.
- No further Experiment 005 rerun is authorised by this result.

## Claim boundary

The result is limited to the two-case synthetic nonlinear central-gravity testbed and the frozen primary-navigation bias/dropout population. It does not qualify flight safety, establish operational prevalence, validate six-degree-of-freedom or hardware-in-the-loop behaviour, or support a learned-policy claim.

Experiment 005 is complete. The programme can now move to final Space Autonomy Lab benchmark, demo, documentation, and release preparation.
