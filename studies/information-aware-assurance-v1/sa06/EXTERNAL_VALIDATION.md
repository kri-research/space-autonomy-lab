# External evidence and test boundary

## Current evidence

On 27 September 2026, the phase records, available connected device inventory
and scoped authorized correspondence searches did not establish access to a
research target processor, calibrated sensor/robotic rig, traceable metrology,
trial permissions or an independently produced integration/replication report.
The connected computer is the originating Darwin arm64 development host. It is
not relabelled as a spacecraft target. Absence of a record within this scoped
inspection does not establish that no such equipment exists elsewhere.

Software installation, the separate consumer process and the local example are
internal integration evidence. Existing host timings and simulation remain their
own evidence classes. No invitations were sent, services purchased, target
measurements invented or physical motion initiated.

## Minimal non-actuating target procedure

Before starting a target measurement, record the device/model, processor/OS,
power mode and thermal state, accessible memory, authorized owner/operator,
source commit and wheel digest, dependencies, exact input identities and interface
boundaries. Confirm the proposed connection has no actuator backend. Freeze a
new procedure and new destination before measurements. A remote interpreter or
serial-port listing alone does not establish the target's identity or suitability.

The packaged CLI uses JSON files/stdout and can be wrapped by a target-local
runner. Start with all ten published examples, one retained warm-up and two
repetitions per input, in three separately launched sessions. Retain every return,
error and timeout. Record complete process startup separately from assessment
roundtrip, include serialization and logging, and capture scheduling and resource
contention under specified idle and approved load conditions. Specify the actual
meaningful target deadline rather than selecting one after observing timings.
Use a single local monotonic clock; cross-device measurements need an explicit
alignment error budget and measurement boundary. Maxima are observations, not WCET.

The built-in `benchmark` is only the fixed one-session host subprocedure. It must
not be reported as the full target procedure above. If physical energy matters,
define the metered rail/system boundary, instrument, calibration, sampling,
alignment and idle baseline before recording; CPU time is not energy. Permission
and source-bound target records remain prerequisites, not prefilled approvals.

## Independent integration

An independent team can provide timestamped range/bearing data in the documented
schema and replace `propose()` in `examples/controller_adapter.py`. The assurance
core is unchanged. Their estimator may inform their proposal; the credited state
set is still reconstructed from the declared observations and initial conditions.
Integration of another estimator as a credited bound needs its own justified
containment contract; this version does not accept arbitrary covariances or
externally supplied certificates.

Record team provenance and consent to reuse their report, date, component/source
and dependency identities, instrument/model assumptions, complete input/output
records, their modifications, failed cases and the support they needed. A report
using modified code must identify that version. Compare every promised status and
scope against the public interface, retaining differences and missing evidence.
A local second process or an author's repeated run remains internal evidence.
No external report is present or claimed in this release.

## Physical tests

The component has no physical actuation driver. Physical trials additionally need
trial-specific permission, a competent operator, independent protection, justified
entry/abort limits, calibrated sensors, command-termination behavior and traceable
metrology. Establish the entire sensing-to-actuation deadline and failure response.
A finite simulated coast is not an indefinite hardware stop or recovery guarantee.
Until those prerequisites and actual records exist, physical evidence stays pending.
