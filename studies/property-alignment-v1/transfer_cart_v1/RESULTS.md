# Prospective lagged-cart transfer results

All128 prespecified inputs completed once under the deposited design, with no
worker failures or missing cases. The numerical design was frozen after the
spacecraft evaluation; the cart family itself had been selected in Task02.

The lag-aware criterion produced77 independently checked finite prefixes,
45 independently checked obstructions and six unresolved outcomes. None of its
definite results was contradicted by the independently implemented referee.
Twenty obstruction cases had a verified prefix for every singleton hypothesis;
nine had verified prefixes for every pair while the joint set had no shared
held command. These concern finite-prefix compatibility, not full recoverability.

The robust predictive-prefix comparator certified79 prefixes, two more than the
candidate. It is therefore not supported that the candidate improves positive
protection coverage. The third-order barrier certified32; its conservative entry
conditions are retained. Mean-only and instantaneous-actuator ablations made64
and41 refuted admissions. These incomplete ablations are not sound baselines.

In the16 prespecified out-of-assumption lag tests, eleven lag-aware commands were
available. Seven violated containment at four times the declared actuator lag,
and four remained contained. Five cases had no candidate command. This failure
of uncalibrated model transfer is reported separately and was not used to retune
any method, threshold, population or existing result.

The standard-library-only isolated export reproduced738 fixed command and dual
checks with zero mismatches and no imports of online controllers or optimizers.
This reproduces numerical evidence using a second formulation; it is not an
independent laboratory experiment, a hardware timing trial or a new test population.

## Permitted generalization

The information-compatible held-command obstruction and checked-prefix method
can be re-derived for this separately implemented actuator-lag model with a new
workspace and observation set. Its physical model and information assumptions
are essential. Literal orbital-code transfer, universal coverage, complete mission
utility, controller superiority and operational safety are not established.

All group-specific and per-case outputs remain in `recorded/`; the pre-execution
source, parameter and input bindings remain in `frozen/`. No original spacecraft
or failed-attempt file was modified, and no transfer root was replaced or added.
